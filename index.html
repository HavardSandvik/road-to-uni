Coming up

    The plan: one application in October 2027, all for 2029 entry

    • Gap year first: a music year at Musikkfolkehøgskolen Viken (2028/29), then university from autumn 2029. Every choice is a deferred application, with 2029 as the entry year on the UCAS form.
    • Cambridge Natural Sciences: deferral is fine. Needs the ESAT in October 2027.
    • Medicine at Barts, Leicester and Keele: all three accept Chemistry with Maths or Physics (no Biology) and consider deferred applications. They need the UCAT in summer 2027. Barts only guarantees deferral if it is stated on UCAS from the start. Confirm Keele's policy by email.
    • Fifth choice: open. A second science course, or another medical school that accepts her subjects and deferral, if one turns up when the 2028-entry policies are published (around summer 2027).
    • UCAS allows at most 4 Medicine/Dentistry choices out of 5, and only one of Oxford or Cambridge. The personal statement must work for both NatSci and Medicine.

    Backup: apply again from Norway in 2028

    • If she has not firmly accepted an offer, she can apply again in autumn 2028 for 2029 entry, with A-level grades in hand. This opens every medical and dental school that doesn't require Biology, including those that don't take deferred applications.
    • That means sitting the UCAT again (summer 2028) and asking Owen's for a reference after leaving. Interviews would fall during the Viken year (December 2028 to March 2029), some of them online.
    • Don't firmly accept a Medicine offer that she might later turn down. Medical schools look badly on applicants who declined a Medicine place.
